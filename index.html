Flagship · MagnaWave

MPower: One Education Brand for Every Audience

When I arrived, the company's education put everyone in one bucket and treated every customer as a practitioner starting a business. The videos were outdated and ran anywhere from 2 to 50 minutes. The results were a wide competence gap, repeated support calls, and practitioners misusing equipment or misrepresenting the brand.

  • Separated learners by purpose, personal use or professional use, and designed education for each group
  • Built MPower as an umbrella that can hold all company education and scale to any audience or brand
  • Worked with marketing and design on MPower's branding and wrote its tagline
  • Owned the full pipeline: storyboards, scripts, SME review, MindSmith builds and Synthesia video
  • 40+ courses launch-ready in about 20 months, soft-launched at MagnaCon with a workshop for about 150 attendees
  • Result: Practitioners said the learning was more engaging and that they gained the confidence they had been missing
